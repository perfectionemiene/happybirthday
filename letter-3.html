A Letter From Havilah

Dear Mommy,

Dear Mummy, Sometimes, I genuinely wonder how one woman can carry so much and still wake up the next morning ready to carry some more. You are a mother of five, but somehow you have managed to become a shelter, a provider, a counsellor, a prayer warrior, a teacher ana a fighter. You have hustled, sacrificed, worried, prayed, worked, stretched what looked impossible, and somehow made a way where there seemed to be absolutely no way. I have watched you give pieces of yourself to make sure your children have what they need, and even when life has not been gentle with you, you have remained standing. You are not perfect, and neither am I, but you are extraordinary in ways words will probably never fully capture. There is something about watching my mother refuse to give up that has taught me that strength is not always loud. Sometimes strength looks like waking up tired and still showing up. Sometimes it looks like tears followed by prayer. Sometimes it looks like saying, “God will provide,” and then going out to do your part while trusting Him to do His. But beyond everything you have done for us, what I admire most is the place God holds in your heart. Your relationship with Him is not something you wear for people to see; it is woven into the way you live, the way you pray, the way you serve, and the way you keep believing even when circumstances give you every reason to be afraid. You have shown me that faith is not merely something we say it is something we live. You have carried your children in prayer, stood in the gap for us, and trusted God with things you could not control. I may not always say it, and I may not always understand every decision you make, but I see it. I see the woman behind the strength. I see the sacrifices behind the smiles. I see the prayers behind the victories. And I pray that the same God you have served with your whole soul will reward you openly for every private sacrifice you have made. And then, of course, there is one of your greatest achievement, You gave birth to the most iconic human to ever grace the earth ✨️HAVILAH. ✨Honestly, Mummy, history may never recover. You didn't just raise five children; you contributed Havilah to humanity, and for that alone, I think heaven owes you a special crown. But seriously, everything I become will always carry traces of you—the resilience, the faith, the ambition, the stubborn determination to keep going, and even the parts of me that are a little too dramatic. I am who I am partly because you were willing to be who you had to be for us. My prayer is that one day, you will sit back and see the harvest of every seed you planted in us. I pray your children become a source of joy, honour, and laughter to you. I pray God gives you strength for every remaining mile, peace for every unanswered question, and blessings that make every sacrifice worth remembering. Mummy, you are a powerful woman, a praying woman, a loving mother, and a woman whose story is still being written. And if I could choose my mother again, with all the knowledge of this life, I would still choose you. Lilian, Perfection, Eternity, Paul and I lovee you very much. Happy birthday to the best mummy in the wholeeeee world! 🥳🎂❤️ Your iconic daughter,

Thank you for everything you do for us every single day.

With all my love,

Havilah❤️
