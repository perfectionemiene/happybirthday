A Letter From Perfection

Dear Mommy,

Happy Birthday, Mommy❤️ You’re a strong woman. I’m sure you don’t even need me to tell you that, but you really are. God has helped you achieve so much, and regardless of what anybody has ever said, YOU’RE A GREAT MOTHER! When the Bible said, “Her children will rise up and call her blessed,” it really didn’t lie. Every single one of us knows how blessed you are, and honestly, we are so blessed to have you as our mother. God knows that if I had all the money in the world, you would never want for anything. You deserve so much. I’ve seen true love, dedication, sacrifice, support, forgiveness, friendship, encouragement and genuineness through your life and your actions. Believe me when I say this: you’re a good woman. And it’s not just with us. You continually help people who have nobody to turn to or rely on. Somehow, you become a mother or a friend to them. You take on other people's responsibilities so effortlessly. If you had ₦10,000 and someone needed it to eat, you would probably give them the entire ₦10,000. 😂 I know it’s something we give you a hard time about, but your kind heart is genuinely admirable. There’s honestly just so much to say. Or is it all the shame you’ve endured because of us? People continually insulting you for taking care of your own children. People chastising you for not allowing us to go and do menial jobs. The things we’ve heard and seen ehn…😂 But God has been faithful. His Word is forever true, and He says He frustrates the devices of the enemy. He has proved that over and over again in our lives. I think I know how easy it is for me to spot a single mother now because I’ve seen firsthand the kind of strength you possess. 😂 All those times when we would all make our hair properly, but you’d braid yours all back and leave it for months because you would rather spend the money on us. Even when you can afford to have the best things, you sacrifice them over and over again for us because that’s simply who you are. You love so selflessly, even people who sometimes don't deserve it. Your heart for God is another thing entirely. Your service to Him. Your heart of worship. Your love for His Word. The way you’ve continually tried to point us towards Christ. There are honestly so many things I could outline, and I’d probably still not be able to say everything. But above all, as a person, God bless you. God bless you for your labour of love for my siblings and I. For choosing us. For teaching us the way of Christ. For encouraging us in our walk with God. For encouraging me whenever I feel insecure. For holding our hands whenever we stumble. You’re a true mother hen.😂❤️ Nobody touches your chicks without feeling your wrath. As a mother nd a father, you’ve protected us countless times. Is it Chike? Isaac? People from TNC? After you na you! 😂😂 You have fought for us, defended us, prayed for us, provided for us and stood by us in ways I can never fully explain. God bless you, Mummy. God bless you over and over again. I know I will never be able to repay you for all the sacrifices you’ve made for us, but God knows I’ll try my very best to make you proud. And yes, I’ll still buy you that private jet and imprint your name on the tail.😂 You deserve it! Happy birthday, Mommy.❤️ I think all of this is just a very long way of saying one thing: I love you. You already know this, but I’m saying it again anyway. I love you so, so much. Thank you for being my mother. Thank you for being you. God bless you, Mummy.🫶🏼❤️

Thank you for everything you do for us every single day.

With all my love,

Perfection❤️
